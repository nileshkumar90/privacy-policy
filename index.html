Legal & Privacy Information

1. Introduction

Welcome to FocusLoop. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your data when you use FocusLoop for timer management, focus sessions, habit tracking, and productivity statistics.

2. Information We Collect

FocusLoop operates on an offline-first local architecture. We prioritize user privacy and minimize data collection:

  • Local Habit & Session Data: Your habit titles, timer presets, completed cycles, and focus duration logs are stored locally on your device using an encrypted local SQLite database (Room DB).
  • Settings & Preferences: Sound settings, vibration preferences, and timer durations are saved strictly on your local device storage.
  • No Personal Tracking: We do NOT track your location, read your contacts, access your media files, or harvest personal identifiable information.

3. How We Use Your Data

All data generated within FocusLoop is used exclusively for:

  • Calculating your daily, weekly, and all-time focus statistics.
  • Managing active Pomodoro focus countdowns and ambient notification sounds.
  • Displaying habit streaks and progress visualizations.
  • Allowing you to export your data in CSV or JSON format anytime.

4. Data Storage & User Rights

You have full ownership and control over all data created in FocusLoop:

  • 100% On-Device Storage: Your data remains solely on your smartphone or tablet.
  • Data Export: You can back up or export your data at any time from the App Settings menu in standard CSV or JSON files.
  • Data Erasure: You can completely clear all data from the device at any time using the "Reset All Data" option in Settings or by clearing the app cache.

5. Third-Party Services & Permissions

FocusLoop requests minimal system permissions:

  • Notifications & Alarms: Required to notify you when focus cycles or breaks complete, even when the app is running in the background.
